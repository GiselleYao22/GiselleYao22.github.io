Research

In progress

Infant Curiosity and Caregiver Speech

Do infants who tend to be more curious hear more speech or a wider range of words from their caregivers during play?

How I study it

I pair caregiver reports of infant curiosity with transcripts of caregiver–infant play. I analyze how much caregivers talk and how many different words they use.

Qualifying project proposal

Caregiver Speech and Facial Expressions

How do caregivers coordinate speech and facial expressions during play?

How I plan to study it

I plan to use video recordings of caregiver–infant play to examine when caregivers’ facial expressions and speech occur together and whether this coordination is related to infants’ vocabulary at 18 months.
